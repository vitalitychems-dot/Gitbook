## Review method for any future dedupe

A duplicate may be retired only when a content hash and Git history establish equivalence, all live imports/routes/build references and runtime consumers are checked, the replacement is authoritative, tests prove behavior, the rollback restores the exact old behavior, and an appropriate reviewed change records the deletion. Archives and provenance copies remain until the repository owner approves retention changes. Filename similarity, historical claims, or a clean build alone are insufficient.
