# Tesla — public-domain patents & methods (Rick knowledge base)
All Tesla US patents are expired (public domain). Source of verification: MIT list (web.mit.edu/most/Public/Tesla1/grant_tesla.html) + Google Patents, checked 2026-10-12.

## Verified (number, grant date, title)
- 381,968 — 1888-05-01 — Electro-Magnetic Motor. Polyphase AC induction motor; rotating magnetic field from phase-shifted currents.
- 381,969 / 382,279 — 1888-05-01 — Electro-Magnetic Motor (variants).
- 382,280 / 382,281 — 1888-05-01 — Electrical Transmission of Power. Polyphase AC transmission system (382,280 carries a disclaimer).
- 512,340 — 1894-01-09 — Coil for Electro-Magnets. Bifilar coil: adjacent turns at high potential difference => raised self-capacitance, cancels self-induction without separate condenser.
- 645,576 — 1900-03-20 — System of Transmission of Electrical Energy. Resonant, high-potential transmitter/receiver via natural media.
- 725,605 — 1903-04-14 — System of Signaling. Multi-frequency tuned circuits: receiver responds only when several tunings coincide (early selectivity/"AND" of channels).
- 787,412 — 1905-04-18 — Art of Transmitting Electrical Energy Through the Natural Mediums. Earth resonance/standing waves.
- 1,119,732 — 1914-12-01 (filed 1902, renewed 1907) — Apparatus for Transmitting Electrical Energy. Large smooth-curvature elevated terminal (Wardenclyffe) to hold charge without leakage.
- 514,167 — Electrical Conductor (early coaxial-type conductor; per Wikipedia list).

## Methods / design principles (useful as invention patterns)
1. Rotating field: several out-of-phase simple signals combine into motion -> stagger parallel workers by phase.
2. Resonance/tuning: drive a system at its natural frequency; small input, large response -> tune batch sizes/cadence to measured optimum.
3. Selectivity by coincidence (725,605): act only when N independent channels agree -> quorum gating.
4. Bifilar self-cancellation (512,340): structure that cancels its own reactance -> paired ops that cancel overhead.
5. Smooth large terminal (1,119,732): avoid sharp edges where leakage occurs -> eliminate edge-case losses.
6. Valvular conduit: flow easy one way, hard the other, no moving parts -> one-way append logs.
7. Mental prototyping: Tesla reportedly built/tested designs mentally before metal -> simulate before implement.
